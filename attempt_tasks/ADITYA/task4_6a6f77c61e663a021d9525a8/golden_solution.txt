WHY THIS INVALIDATES THE READ (state the mechanism, do not just assert the verdict)
Users whose devices were excluded cannot install. Users who cannot install cannot convert. Every excluded impression still lands in the denominator of CVR and in the numerator of CPI. The result is suppressed CVR and inflated CPI regardless of which creative was served. Whatever real difference existed between creatives is swamped by an eligibility filter that applied identically to all of them.
The true excluded-device share for Brazil is NOT established inside the window. It stays unknown. Do not import a percentage to fill this gap.
